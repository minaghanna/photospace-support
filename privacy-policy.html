PhotoSpace Privacy Policy

Updated November 22, 2026 · Developer: Mina Hanna

PhotoSpace uses the Photos access you grant to measure file sizes, find exact copies and preview or apply edits you choose. With limited access, only the media you select is available. Scanning and comparison happen on your device. PhotoSpace has no advertising or accounts. Photos, videos, captions, filenames, file sizes, scan results and cleanup history stay on your device.

The app keeps a local scan index of asset identifiers, measurements, timestamps and file fingerprints. Its local cleanup history contains dates, actions, byte counts and hashed asset identifiers. It uses temporary media files when preparing edits. Deleting the app removes its local data. Items you saved to Photos remain in your Photos library.

You can change or revoke access in Settings › Apps › PhotoSpace › Photos. Deleted originals move to Recently Deleted in Photos, where you can recover them before permanent removal. Apple manages iCloud Photos downloads, syncing and any system diagnostics you choose to share.

Optional usage statistics

PhotoSpace uses Google Analytics for Firebase for optional usage statistics. In TestFlight build 15, usage statistics are on by default for people who have not previously made a sharing choice. Existing choices carry forward. In TestFlight build 14 and in the App Store version, collection starts only after you explicitly opt in. You can turn off Share usage statistics at any time in Privacy & Support. Reports help the developer understand feature use and cleanup outcomes. They include app activity, categories for features and cleanup outcomes, app version and build, and an app-instance identifier. Google also collects standard app and device information, session activity and general location derived from masked IP addresses. PhotoSpace disables advertising identifier collection and advertising personalization. Reports exclude photos, videos, captions, filenames, Photos identifiers, file sizes, bytes saved, scan results and raw error messages.

Firebase may store reports on the device for delivery when it is online. Turning off sharing in Privacy & Support stops further collection and resets local analytics data. It does not erase reports already sent to Google. For questions about retention or requests to delete previously sent data, use the support form below. See Google’s privacy policy.

For help or privacy requests, use the shared support form and mention PhotoSpace. The developer uses the information you submit to respond, keeps it as needed to resolve the request, and can be asked to delete it. GitHub hosts this page, and Google hosts the support form. Opening them shares ordinary connection information with the respective provider.
